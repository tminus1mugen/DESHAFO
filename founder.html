Founder & Executive Director

Rolex Ong'ute

MSc · Teacher · Mentor · Child Protection Advocate

Rolex Akinyi Ong'ute is a passionate educator, community leader, and philanthropist dedicated to transforming the lives of children, youth, and families through education, mentorship, and social empowerment.

She currently serves as a teacher of Physics and Chemistry at Obera Boys High School, where she has consistently inspired learners to overcome fear of science subjects and achieve academic excellence through practical, learner-centered approaches. With over 15 years of teaching experience, she is deeply committed to nurturing critical thinking, confidence, and purpose among her students.

Rolex is the Founder and Executive Director of DESHAFO Integrated CBO and DESHAFO Integrated School, institutions that provide holistic support to underprivileged children, teenagers, and women. Through her leadership, the organization has become a beacon of hope in the community, offering mentorship, education support, and empowerment programs that address real-life challenges facing vulnerable groups.

She is the convener of the annual GRETECHI (Great Teens and Children) Conference, a flagship mentorship initiative that has impacted hundreds of young people each year for over 15 years. The program focuses on academic growth, life skills, health awareness, moral values, and social well-being, contributing significantly to positive behavioral change and strengthened family relationships.

In recognition of her outstanding contribution to community development and philanthropy, Rolex was honored with the 2025 Presidential Heroes Award (Shujaa) under the Philanthropy Category in Homa Bay County.

Beyond her work in education and community service, Rolex is a certified Children and Family Mediator with the Kenyan courts, supporting conflict resolution and family restoration. She also contributes to national education development as a Chemistry panelist with KICD, helping shape quality curriculum materials.

She holds a Master's Degree in Business Administration from Maseno University, which enhances her leadership, strategic planning, and organizational management in both educational and community initiatives. Rolex is also a regular speaker on Ramogi TV and Radio, where she addresses issues related to parenting, education, and child protection. In addition, she serves as a Children's Minister at MOSA Church, Homa Bay, where she mentors young people in spiritual growth and character development.

"To help children, teens, and youth identify their inbuilt potential, amplify their voices, and become future leaders and change-makers in their communities."

Chairman

Apostle Ben Okello

Spiritual Leader · International Speaker · Mentor

Apostle Ben Okello is the spiritual leader and founder of MOSA Churches Kenya, a ministry with a growing national and international presence. His pastoral work spans decades of faithful service, guiding congregations through practical faith, discipleship, and community-centered ministry.

As a renowned international speaker, Apostle Ben carries a message of holistic empowerment — one that addresses the spiritual, social, and economic needs of individuals and families. He has spoken at conferences and crusades across East Africa, inspiring thousands to pursue purpose-driven lives.

As Chairman of DESHAFO, he provides the moral compass and spiritual foundation that underpins every program, ensuring the organization's work is grounded in integrity, compassion, and sustainable community transformation.

"True empowerment begins from within — when communities are spiritually grounded, every other transformation becomes possible."

Vice Chairman

Julius Oracha Omuga

Community Leader · Former Teacher · Climate Advocate

Julius Oracha Omuga is a respected grassroots mobilizer with deep roots in education and community service. As a former teacher, he brings a genuine understanding of the challenges facing youth and families in rural communities — and a hands-on commitment to addressing them.

Julius has been instrumental in advancing education access, mentorship programs, and sustainable livelihood initiatives across the region. His network within local communities enables DESHAFO to reach the most vulnerable families with targeted, meaningful support.

He is also a passionate advocate for climate awareness and environmental stewardship, championing tree planting, water conservation, and eco-friendly practices as essential pillars of long-term community wellbeing in the Lake Victoria region.

"Building resilient communities means investing in people, land, and opportunity — all at once, all together."

Treasurer & Head of Operations

Conslate Okello

Financial Manager · Accountant · Operations Lead

Conslate Okello holds a Diploma in Financial Management from the Kenya Institute of Management and brings both technical expertise and a deep sense of mission to her role. As Treasurer and Head of Operations, she is the backbone of DESHAFO's financial health and day-to-day functioning.

She oversees budgeting, financial reporting, procurement, and accountability frameworks that ensure every shilling donated reaches its intended purpose. Her diligence and transparency have been central to building donor trust and securing continued support for DESHAFO's programs.

Beyond financial management, Conslate actively supports community empowerment initiatives on the ground — embodying the belief that strong organizational systems are what allow meaningful human impact to scale and endure.

"Accountability is not just a financial term — it is the promise we make to every family that trusts us with their future."
