What We Do

Our Core Areas of Impact

DESHAFO integrates several community-driven activities under one mission — transforming lives from the roots up.

Education & Mentorship

Empowering the younger generation to identify their potential and academic pathways through the CBC curriculum.

Climate Action

Combating climate change through tree planting, environmental education, and sustainable farming — empowering Kenyan youth as environmental stewards in Homabay and beyond.

Teen Mother Support

Counselling and reintegration of teen mothers back into school and community life with dignity and care.

GRETECHI Conference

Annual flagship event each August — covering spirituality, SGBV awareness, talent development, and CBC pathways.

About DESHAFO

DEstiny SHApers FOundation

Founded in 2019 by Rolex Ong'ute, DESHAFO Integrated CBO is a registered community-based organization in Outbram Village, Central Ward, Homabay Town Sub-county.

Our mission is to create opportunities for underprivileged children, youth, and women through education, mentorship, and climate action. We also operate DESHAFO Integrated School — a testament to our commitment to accessible education.

Meet Our Founder

Rolex Ong'ute

Founder & Executive Director · Physics & Chemistry Teacher · Child Protection Advocate

Rolex Ong'ute is a passionate educator, community leader, and philanthropist dedicated to transforming the lives of children, youth, and families through education, mentorship, and social empowerment. With over 15 years of teaching experience, she inspires learners to overcome fear of science subjects and achieve academic excellence. She holds a Master's degree from Maseno University and teaches at Obera Boys High School. As Children's Minister at MOSA Church and convener of the annual GRETECHI Conference, she brings faith, resilience, and compassionate leadership to every initiative.

"To help children, teens, and youth identify their inbuilt potential, amplify their voices, and become future leaders and change-makers in their communities."
